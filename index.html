What's inside

Everything a focus session needs, nothing it doesn't

Pomodoro timer

Work in timed focus sessions with short breaks in between, so you get more done without burning out.

Tasks with due dates

Add what you need to get done, give it a due date and pick the one to focus on next.

Focus stats

See how much time you've spent focusing and watch your habit build.

How it works

Three steps to a better study session

  1. 1

    Pick a task

    Choose what you're working on from your task list, or add a new one.

  2. 2

    Start the timer

    Focus until the session ends. Put the phone down and let FocusBull keep time.

  3. 3

    Take a real break

    Rest, then go again. Your sessions add up in Stats.
