Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. We collect only the information necessary to power your focus workflow and improve the experience safely.

Last Updated: September 12, 2026

1.Introduction

Focus Bull ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and disclose information when you use our mobile application.

2.Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: When you create an account, we collect your email address and password via Firebase Authentication.
  • User Content: We store your tasks, focus session history, and productivity statistics in Google Firebase Firestore and locally on your device using Room.
  • Usage Data: We use Google Analytics for Firebase to collect anonymous usage data (e.g., app opens, feature usage) to help us improve the app.
  • Media Files: If you choose to set a custom background, the app accesses local storage to display your selected image. We do not upload your personal images to our servers.

3.How We Use Information

  • To provide and maintain the service (syncing your tasks).
  • To personalize your experience (background settings).
  • To monitor app performance and fix bugs.

4.Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party providers who may collect data according to their own policies:

  • Google Play Services
  • Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Analytics)

5.Data Security

We value your trust. Your account data is secured by Firebase's industry-standard encryption. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

6.Your Choices

You can update your account information or delete your account at any time through the app settings.
